# Design System

## Brand & Style
The design style follows a modern, clean, and highly reliable corporate aesthetic. It leverages **Inter** for precise readability across all viewports, combined with a structured layout and balanced whitespace. The visual tone is professional, trustworthy, and clear.

## Colors
The color palette is built for clarity and impact, using a crisp primary surface (`#ffffff`), a vibrant secondary blue (`#0458e9`) for focal interactive elements, and an energetic tertiary green (`#12ff39`) for high-contrast accents and status indicators.

## Typography
Typography relies entirely on **Inter** to ensure maximum legibility and a contemporary look. The scale provides distinct steps from large headers down to concise UI labels.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid grid system is used with standard 1rem gutters and 1.5rem outer margins. The spacing scale (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) dictates consistent padding and gaps across all standard components.

## Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy is achieved through tonal layers and clean, low-contrast outlines rather than heavy drop shadows, preserving a flat yet structured modern aesthetic.

## Shapes
The design uses a rounded shape language (`roundedness: 2`), featuring a base 0.5rem corner radius for standard containers and buttons, scaling up to 1rem for large cards and modals to create a friendly, approachable UI.

## Components
Components are styled to align with the Inter typography and rounded shape language. Buttons, inputs, and cards feature 0.5rem to 1rem rounded corners and make strong use of the secondary blue and tertiary accent colors for states.
